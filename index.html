let you go

i'm trying to let you go,
my friends say i look distracted these days.
i'm trying to let you go
(your smile, your words,your gaze).

here i am,
writing for you in my notebook again.
darling, i can't tell if i'm trying to forget
or just going once more insane.

trying to forget you just feels like
i'm falling in love again.
